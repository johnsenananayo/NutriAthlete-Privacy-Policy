About this policy

NutriAthlete (“the app”) is a nutrition tracking app for student-athletes. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Information you enter in the app

The app stores the following information only on your device:

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password, used only to log in on your device.
  • Athlete profile: sport or activity, age, weight, and height.
  • Nutrition goals: daily calorie, protein, and water targets.
  • Records you create: meals, calories, protein, water intake, meal plans, meal and water reminder schedules, weight entries, and daily progress.
  • Photos: if you add a meal photo, the app keeps a reference to it on your device so it can show the photo in your meal record.
  • Preferences: for example, light or dark mode.

This information is kept in the app’s private storage and is used only to provide the app’s features, such as daily totals, progress, and reminders.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number, device identifiers, advertising ID, or browsing activity. The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not show advertising.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the meal, water, and progress reminders you set. You can turn them off in your phone settings.
  • Vibration and restart events: so reminders can vibrate and are restored after your phone restarts.
  • Camera and photos: only if you choose to take or select a meal photo. The app uses your phone’s own camera or photo picker and has no continuous access to your camera or gallery.

Sharing and third parties

The app does not share your data with anyone and does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or data services. If you tap Share (for example, to share a weekly nutrition summary), the app opens your phone’s share menu with text you can review. It goes only to the app and person you choose, and the developer does not receive it.

Data retention and how to delete your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it in any of these ways:

  • In the app: open Profile and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and the nutrition, profile, reminder, and progress data saved by the app.
  • In your phone settings: open the app’s storage settings and choose Clear storage.
  • Uninstall the app.
Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing stored elsewhere to delete. Original photos in your phone’s gallery are not removed by deleting your account.

Data security

Your data is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot normally access. No method of storage is completely secure, so please protect your device with a screen lock and avoid reusing a password you use elsewhere.

Children

NutriAthlete is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under the age of majority in your country, please use the app with a parent or guardian.

Health information and medical disclaimer

The app lets you record information such as weight, height, and nutrition. This information stays on your device. NutriAthlete provides general nutrition tracking and educational information only. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

Please speak to a doctor or qualified dietitian before changing your diet or training, especially if you have a health condition.

Your rights

Because all your data is on your device, you can view, edit, and delete it directly in the app. You may contact us at any time with questions about this policy or your privacy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this web address and the effective date will be updated. Please check it from time to time.
